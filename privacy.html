Privacy Policy

Social Vortex · last updated 10 October 2026

Social Vortex is a personal application used only by its owner to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts. It has no other users.

What data it accesses

  • Basic account information (channel or account name and ID), to show which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos and publish posts on the connected accounts.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to upload and publish videos that the owner chooses.
  • Login tokens, videos and settings are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • Data obtained through Google APIs is handled in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google account (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or in Instagram's settings under Apps and websites. Deleting the application's local data folder removes everything it stored.
